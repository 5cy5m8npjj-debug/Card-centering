Your photo stays on your device. For accuracy, manually place all eight guide handles. Irregular/borderless card designs may not have a meaningful rectangular centering ratio.

How to use

1. Tap Take / Choose Photo.
2. Put the green corners on the physical card corners.
3. Put the red corners on the inner printed frame/border.
4. Read the live L/R and T/B percentages.
5. Switch to Back and repeat.

Starting Guides only places the boxes near typical locations; you should fine-tune them manually.
